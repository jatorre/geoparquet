[[ats_distribution_row-group-size]]
[abstract_test]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /conf/distribution/row-group-size
target:: /req/distribution/row-group-size
test-purpose:: Verify the row group sizes.
test-method::
+
--
1. If the total size of the file is below 64 MB and it has one row group, the test passes.

2. Otherwise, for every row group, verify that `num_rows` is at least 10 000 and at most 200 000.

NOTE: Implemented by `gpio check row-group` and `gpio check optimization` (factor 4); the thresholds in this Standard are those of geoparquet-io and are to be confirmed by the community.
--
====
