[[req_core_orientation-rings]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/core/orientation-rings
part:: If the `orientation` member of a column metadata object is present, the vertices of the exterior ring of every Polygon in the geometry column, including Polygons that are members of a MultiPolygon or of a GeometryCollection, SHALL be ordered counterclockwise, and the vertices of every interior ring SHALL be ordered clockwise.
====
